1.  INTRODUCTION 
The objective of this RFP is to empanel a panel of three (3) Creative Media Agencies to develop and 
implement the overall communication strategy for the Invest Telangana - Global Summit and for any 
other event or program in future as decided by authority. The empaneled agencies shall be responsible 
for creative execution across various media, media planning and implementation, film and content 
production, social media management and digital amplification to communicate the Summit's vision, 
investment opportunities and key messages effectively to relevant audiences. 
1.1 Background and Rationale 
Invest Telangana, is organizing the Global Summit as a flagship platform to showcase the State's in-
vestment climate, industrial strengths and developmental achievements to domestic and international 
audiences. Invest Telangana recognized the need to empanel a panel of professionally qualified Crea-
tive Media Agencies (each bidder being an entity or a permitted Consortium) capable of planning, cre-
ating and executing an integrated communication campaign across print, electronic, digital, social me-
dia and influencer platforms for the Summit. The work to build Telangana as an investment destina-
tion will be a continuous process and will need communication from time to time.  
1.2 Nature of the Empanelment/Engagement 
This RFP contemplates the empanelment of three (3) Creative Media Agencies/Consortia to support 
the Summit, selected through Pre-Qualification, Technical Evaluation (on a Qualify/Disqualify basis, 
subject to the minimum qualifying marks prescribed in Clause 4.3) and ranking of the Financial Pro-
posals of technically qualified Bidders in ascending order of the quoted Fixed Monthly Retainer Fee, 
the three lowest-quoting technically qualified Bidders being designated L1 (lowest), L2 (second-
lowest) and L3 (third-lowest) respectively and empaneled. Upon empanelment, work may be allocated 
among the three empaneled Agencies in ratio of (5:3:2) at L1 prices at the sole discretion of the Au-
thority, depending on the nature, scale and requirement of each activity; mere empanelment shall not 
guarantee any minimum quantum of work to an empaneled agency. The engagement follows the broad 
governance principles commonly adopted in State-level empanelment frameworks for media, social 
media and influencer-marketing agencies, including defined proposal validity, Earnest Money Deposit 
and Performance Security requirements, standards of performance, penalty and termination provisions, 
and confidentiality obligations, so as to ensure transparency, accountability and value for money in the 
selection and management of the empaneled Agencies. 
Among the three (3) empaneled Agencies, the Authority shall, on the basis of their demonstrated     
capability, designate one (1) Agency as the lead coordinating Member and the remaining two (2) 
Agencies as Supporting Agencies for the purpose of coordinating and executing the Summit's commu-
nication activities. The Authority reserves the right to review and re-designate this arrangement at any 
time during the term of empanelment, as it may consider necessary.

2.  SCOPE OF WORK 
The empaneled agencies shall undertake the following activities for the Invest Telangana-Global 
Summit and other flagship programs and projects of Govt of Telangana: 
2.1 Communication Strategy 
● Develop the overall communication strategy, campaign themes, key messages and content 
plan. 
● Identify relevant audiences, sectors and markets, and recommend suitable communication 
channels. 
● Prepare an integrated campaign calendar covering pre-event, during-event and post-event 
communication. 
2.2 Creative Development and Execution 
● Conceptualize and develop campaign creatives for print, outdoor, television, radio, digital, so-
cial media and other approved media. 
● Develop campaign taglines, key visuals, advertisements, brochures, presentations and other 
communication materials. 
● Adapt approved creatives into relevant languages, sizes and formats, ensuring consistency in 
branding and messaging. 
2.3 Media Planning and Implementation 
● Prepare media plans recommending suitable platforms, publications, channels, locations and 
campaign schedules. 
● Coordinate and implement approved media activities, including bookings, placements and re-
leases, within approved budgets. 
● Monitor execution and submit supporting records of media deployment and campaign perfor-
mance. 
2.4 Film and Content Production 
● Undertake research, scripting, storyboarding, filming, photography, editing and post-
production for approved content. 
● Produce promotional films, sector-focused videos, interviews, testimonials, short videos, reels, 
motion graphics and other audiovisual content. 
● Provide photography and videography coverage of the Summit and related activities, including 
timely content for ongoing and post-event communication. 
● Prepare language and format adaptations, including subtitles, dubbing and platform-specific 
edits, as required.

2.5 Social Media Management 
● Manage designated social media handles, including content planning, creation, scheduling and 
publishing. 
● Develop platform-specific static posts, videos, reels, infographics, stories and carousels. 
● Undertake community management, respond to queries in consultation with the Department, 
and monitor trends, conversations and sentiment. 
● Provide timely updates during the Summit and support crisis communication through approved 
messaging. 
2.6 Digital Amplification 
● Plan and execute digital promotion through approved advertising platforms, digital publica-
tions and other relevant channels. 
● Distribute and amplify campaign content through relevant digital networks, communities and 
content platforms. 
● Identify and engage creators and influencers with relevant niche expertise and audience pro-
files to communicate the Summit's themes and investment opportunities. 
● Coordinate creator briefs, participation, content development, approvals, publishing and per-
formance reporting. 
● Monitor and optimize digital promotion and distribution to improve relevant reach and en-
gagement. 
2.7 Reporting and Documentation 
● Submit periodic reports covering activities undertaken, content produced, media deployment, 
digital performance and creator engagement. 
● Track reach, impressions, engagement, video views and advertising effectiveness, as applica-
ble. 
● Maintain records of approvals and execution and submit a final campaign report along with 
creative assets, source files and relevant campaign data. 
2.8 Note on Pricing of Scope of Work 
The Fixed Monthly Retainer Fee quoted under Section 5 of this RFP covers Campaign Media Strategy 
and Media Planning/Buying Advisory Services (Clauses 2.1 and 2.3) only. All other components of 
this Scope of Work — including Creative Development and Execution (2.2), Film and Content Pro-
duction (2.4), Social Media Management (2.5) and Digital Amplification (2.6) — do not form part of 
the Financial Proposal evaluated at the empanelment stage. These components shall be costed, ap-
proved and paid for separately through Work Orders issued after empanelment, in accordance with 
Clause 5.5.
